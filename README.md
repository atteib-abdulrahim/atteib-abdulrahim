# Atteib Abdulrahim

M2 computer science at Sorbonne University, Paris. Systems and high-performance computing.

Most of what I do runs close to the hardware: kernel modules, compute kernels, distributed
services. The part I actually like is the measuring. Profile something, change it, measure
again, and find out you were wrong about where the time was going.

At the moment I'm comparing compiler optimisation levels and instruction sets on compute
kernels, hand-vectorising with AVX-512, and running things across a cluster with OpenMP
and MPI.

**Tools.** C, C++, Python, Bash. Linux, Kali on my own machine. gcc, Clang, GDB, Valgrind,
CMake, Git. OpenMP, MPI, CUDA, SLURM, Docker.

**Security.** Self-taught, on TryHackMe and the PortSwigger Web Security Academy. I play
CTFs, mostly OSINT and exploitation. ANSSI SecNumAcadémie certificate.

**Before university.** I started a small hand-sanitiser distribution business at 18, at the
beginning of the pandemic, and ran it for a year. Two summers of warehouse work after that.

**Off the keyboard.** Middle-distance runner, in a club. I raced the 5000 m at the French
university championships. It is the same habit as the rest: you don't get faster by feeling
faster, you get faster by reading your splits.

**Looking for** a six-month final-year internship from March 2027, in performance
engineering, code optimisation or parallel computing. Paris.
